Curiosity
looks good on you.

What does “self-hosting” mean?

It means running an application or service on hardware you control, rather than relying solely on a hosted service. That might be a computer at home or a rented server.

Do I need technical experience?

A little curiosity helps, but you can start by exploring projects and learning their requirements. The right first project depends on your comfort level and what you want to host.

Is self-hosting automatically more secure?

No. Running a service yourself means you’re responsible for keeping it updated, protecting access, and maintaining backups. Understand the risks before putting sensitive data or services online.
